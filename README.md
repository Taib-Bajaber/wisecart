## 🛒 What is WiseCart?

**WiseCart** is a modern supermarket price-comparison platform designed to help people find better prices without checking every supermarket themselves.

Search for a product, compare verified prices, see availability, and choose where to shop.

### The experience is simple:

**Search → Compare → Save**

---

## 🔎 Search for Anything

Search for products such as:

- Rice
- Milk
- Bread
- Cooking oil
- Sugar
- Flour
- Drinks
- Snacks
- Household products
- Personal care products

WiseCart should make finding a product feel fast and effortless.

## 💰 Compare Prices

WiseCart brings supermarket prices together in one place.

Example:

| Supermarket | Price |
|---|---:|
| Carrefour | **KSh 135** |
| Naivas | KSh 142 |
| Quickmart | KSh 149 |
| FoodPlus | KSh 155 |

The cheapest verified price should always be easy to identify.

---

## 🏪 Supermarkets

WiseCart is designed to support major supermarkets in Kenya.

### Current target stores

- Carrefour
- Naivas
- Quickmart
- Chandarana FoodPlus

More supermarkets can be added as WiseCart grows.

> **Important:** WiseCart must never invent prices. If verified information is unavailable, it should be shown as unavailable.
